Privacy Policy

Effective: 25 September 2026

Chandni Silk Mills uses this catalog and WhatsApp to answer product enquiries, share fabric designs and rates, prepare quotations and orders, and provide customer support.

Information we process

When you contact us, we may process your WhatsApp phone number, profile name, messages, product interests, requested quantities, delivery details, and order or quotation information. Our catalog also records basic usage events such as design views and WhatsApp enquiry clicks.

How we use information

We use this information to respond to enquiries, recommend relevant designs, provide rates and availability, prepare quotations and orders, deliver customer support, prevent abuse, and improve our service. Automated systems may help select catalog products or draft replies, with human assistance available on request.

Service providers

We use Meta WhatsApp Business Platform, n8n Cloud, Cloudflare, and other providers needed to operate messaging, automation, hosting, storage, and security. These providers process information according to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Retention and security

We retain customer and conversation information only as long as needed for sales, support, accounting, legal, fraud-prevention, and operational purposes. We restrict administrative access and use reasonable technical measures to protect stored data.

Your choices

You may ask for a human representative, ask us to stop automated replies, or request access, correction, or deletion of your information, subject to legal record-keeping requirements. Send the request through WhatsApp to +91 95370 97267.

Updates

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
