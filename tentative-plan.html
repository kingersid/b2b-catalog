WhatsApp AI Agent — Tentative Plan

A practical path from the current n8n catalog bot to a dependable sales assistant.

Working direction

Keep the current n8n workflow as the live integration while the agent brain is validated. The model interprets messages and chooses intent; workflow code remains responsible for catalog truth, saved prices, WhatsApp delivery, deduplication, and human escalation.

1. Target flow

Customer WhatsApp→Meta webhook→n8n parse + dedupe→GPT-5.6 brain→Catalog tools→Verified reply
Price rule: the model never invents or supplies rates. n8n reads the private rate and attaches it only after a design has been selected from the active catalog.

2. Agent responsibilities

Low-risk automation

  • Greetings and language matching
  • Catalog browsing and design recommendations
  • Clarifying fabric, colour, use case, or quantity
  • Share active design images and links

Human approval

  • Negotiated prices and discounts
  • Stock reservations and delivery promises
  • Credit, payment, complaints, and final orders
  • Anything uncertain or outside catalog data

3. Delivery phases

0Now

Brain in shadow mode

Add OpenAI Responses API, structured JSON decisions, failure fallback, and logs. Keep existing catalog replies available.

1Next

Catalog-aware replies

Use active designs and saved prices as deterministic workflow data. Add multilingual prompts and intent coverage.

2After validation

Human handoff

Mute automation, notify the operator, preserve context, and resume only after the conversation is released.

3Later

Business tools

Add structured customer records, quote drafts, availability checks, approved templates, and reporting.

4. Suggested decision contract

DecisionWorkflow action
show_designsAllow up to three IDs, validate them against active priced designs, then send images with verified rates.
clarifyAsk one short question about fabric, colour, quantity, or general requirement.
handoffStop automated sales replies and tell the customer a team member will help.
stopRecord opt-out and send no further automated messages.

5. Guardrails to verify

  • OpenAI key exists only in n8n credentials or environment secrets.
  • Prompt injection in customer messages cannot change the agent rules.
  • Only active, positively priced designs can be sent.
  • Saved prices never enter the model prompt.
  • OpenAI outage falls back to the existing catalog response.
  • Meta message IDs are deduplicated before processing.
  • Human requests and complaints always reach an operator.
  • Every model decision and tool result is auditable.

6. Success criteria

Quality

Recommendations reference real active designs, with zero invented prices, availability, or delivery commitments.

Operations

A human can take over quickly, and the bot continues working during model or catalog failures.

Business

Customers get useful answers faster, while quotes and orders remain under business control.
